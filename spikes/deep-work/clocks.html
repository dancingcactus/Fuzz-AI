Deep Work clocks: four ways to see time

One session drives all four. They move in 5-minute steps, never seconds, and none needs you to read a number to know where you are. Play it fast, or drag the slider to any moment, and notice which one you can read in a half-second glance.

1 The shrinking wedge

How much is left in this block, as a slice that gets eaten. Green while you’re on a break.

Reads as a shape, no mathThe best-proven form for time blindnessOnly this block: you can’t see the session or the next break

2 The session track

The whole plan as one bar. Work blocks, break notches, and a marker for where you are. With no end time it grows instead: everything you’ve done so far, plus the next break.

3 Ambient drift

No clock. The frame warms through the block, and one phrase says where you are. With no end time the whole panel gets a little deeper each hour, even after a break.

4 Fuzz is the clock

Fuzz’s energy runs down with the block. It perks up after a break, and gets sleepy as the session ends. With no end time, each break perks it up a little less.

1 + 2 The session dial

The wedge, but for the whole session. Work is orange, breaks are green, and the pie is eaten up to the hand. With no end time it fills up instead: one lap is an hour, the next break waits ahead as a dashed slice, and each finished hour adds a ring outside.

How to compare them

  • The half-second test. Look away, then glance at each for a moment. Which one told you where you are without reading?
  • The 2pm test. You have a meeting in 20 minutes. Which one tells you whether to start the next task?
  • The ambush test. Drag to two minutes before a break. Which one let you see it coming?
  • The open-session test. Pick “Until I stop” and drag to 2 hours. Which one makes it obvious you’ve been at it that long?
  • The flow test. Set real time and work on something else for ten minutes with this open. Which one pulled your eye too often?
  • They aren’t exclusive. The session dial is 1 and 2 combined; the ambient frame could sit on top of any of them.
