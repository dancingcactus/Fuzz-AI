Deep Work: a mode for doing, separate from triage

Triage decides. Deep Work does. When triage is clear, Fuzz hands you a task it has already warmed up, shows you one step at a time, keeps you company, and holds everything else back. Nothing is saved; Reset starts over.

What this spike is testing

  • One decision to start. Fuzz picks the task and pre-selects a 25-minute block. Enter starts it. You can still choose something else, but nothing makes you.
  • Starting is the hard part, so Fuzz does it. Every task in Up next arrives with sources gathered, the first step written as a 5–10 minute action, and a draft to push against.
  • One step on screen. The rest of the plan is folded away. The dots only fill up.
  • No ticking seconds. The clock reads “about 12 min in” and can be hidden. Time running out is a check-in, not an alarm, and “On a roll” is always the first choice (hyperfocus is allowed).
  • Stray thoughts don’t break the flow. T parks one without leaving, and parked thoughts land in Triage afterwards.
  • Stuck has a way out other than quitting. Make the step smaller, let Fuzz take a first pass, skip ahead, or switch to something lighter.
  • Everything about the task is one tap away, and folded by default. People, Sources, Context (Fuzz’s brief) and History sit under the step, so you never leave Deep Work to go digging, but they don’t compete with the step until you open them.
  • Ask Fuzz instead of searching. Q asks a question about the task. Fuzz looks through the sources and answers with where it found it. If the answer isn’t there, Fuzz offers to draft the question to the person who would know; the draft waits in Triage for your OK.
  • Leaving is cheap, and so is coming back. “Done for now” saves where you stopped. The task goes back to the top of Up next with that note on it. Try “Outline the Q3 planning doc” to see a resume.

Open questions

  • Is Up next capped at 3 the right shape, or should Deep Work start straight from Lineup’s “Yours”?
  • Should cards held during Deep Work break through when they’re urgent (a draft due in an hour)?
  • Quiet or chatty Fuzz while you work? Does a visible Fuzz help as a body double or pull your eye?
  • What does Fuzz really need to prep a task well, and how long does that take before it says “Ready”?
  • Should a question Fuzz can’t answer pause the step, or let you keep going while it waits for a reply?
  • Should the context panel sit beside the step on wide screens, or stay folded under it?
  • Does “Done for now” need the note at all, or is Fuzz’s guess (the step you were on) enough?
