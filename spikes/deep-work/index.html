Deep Work: a mode for doing, separate from triage

Triage decides, once or twice a day. Deep Work does, whenever you have time. You say how long and where to start, Fuzz plans the breaks in, and you work one task at a time until you stop. Tasks that stall or keep getting passed over go back to Triage with a fix. Nothing is saved; Reset starts over.

What this spike is testing

  • Triage isn’t a gate. Clearing it is its own win, with a celebration and one quiet line offering Deep Work. The Deep Work button (D) works any time, triage clear or not.
  • The pool is Fuzz’s call. Deep work holds tasks that need you and take real time. Small things stay in Triage; things Fuzz can do, it does.
  • Say how long and where to start, on one screen. Pick up where you left off comes first, then up to three tasks Fuzz has ready (marked by whether they fit your time), then search, or name something new and Fuzz warms it up on the spot.
  • Breaks are planned into the time you give. 25 minutes on, 5 off, a longer break after three. A break waits for the end of a step, up to 5 minutes. “Five more minutes” works once; the second time it’s “skip this one”.
  • Every time interruption takes over the screen. Break due, the break (which shows where you’ll pick up, so “I’m back” goes straight to work), time’s up, and the body check. Keys are ignored for a second so a stray keystroke can’t answer for you. Urgent cards use the same takeover in a different color (try “Send an urgent card”).
  • A coarse clock. “About 12 min in · ~38 left” and a slow bar. It says “Break after this step” when one is waiting.
  • One task at a time until you stop. Finishing gets a win beat, then Fuzz offers one next thing. Enter goes, O picks something else, Esc stops.
  • Stuck tasks go back to Triage with a fix. Stalled (Stuck twice, switched away, or two sessions with no step done) comes back with a proposed split. Passed over (offered four times, never picked) comes back with a smaller first step, a hand-off, or drop it, with no guilt. Pick “Outline the Q3 planning doc” and leave without doing a step to see a stalled card; the rubric shows up as passed over after your first session.

Open questions

  • Is 25/5 the right rhythm, or should it learn from when you actually take breaks?
  • Should a takeover ever wait longer than 5 minutes for the end of a step?
  • How many times does Fuzz offer a task before “passed over” is fair? Four is a guess.
  • Should the stalled and passed-over cards jump to the front of Triage, or wait their turn?
  • Quiet or chatty Fuzz while you work? Does a visible Fuzz help as a body double or pull your eye?
  • What does Fuzz really need to prep a task well, and how many tasks should it keep warm at once?
