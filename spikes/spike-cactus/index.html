Spike, Fuzz's cactus friend

Four looks on one rig, so every move plays on all of them at once. Spike carries his own colours and needs nothing from the app's CSS, so he can move to a personal site as he is. Click any Spike to make him wave. A one-shot goes back to whatever loop was playing.

Open the Potted studio to explore every move: play, pause, scrub, step and tune.

With Fuzz

Spike sits beside Fuzz on the Triage perch. Spike is about the same height as Fuzz's head, so the card doesn't move down. Use the moves above; Fuzz's mood follows along.

Small sizes

The icon sizes use a square crop. At 16 px the shape and the flower have to carry it, because the face is gone.

On a personal site

A rough header and hero to judge Spike as a site mascot. The page colours are Fuzz's warm foundation; Spike dances in the hero.

Notes

  • One rig, four looks. Each look draws its own body, arms and base into the same named groups. The moves are pure CSS keyframes in spike.css, one per group, with pivots in viewBox px set per look.
  • No dependencies. No anime.js and no app CSS. The only id is Potted's clip path, numbered per Spike. spike.css and spike.js are enough to put Spike on any page.
  • Family resemblance. Same face as Fuzz (tall rounded eyes, rosy cheeks, the small smile), Fuzz's terracotta for his shoes and pot, and Fuzz's glow yellow at the heart of the flower.
  • Tempo. The dance runs on one beat variable, --sp-beat, so it could be matched to music.
