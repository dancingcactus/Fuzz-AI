Schedule for later: picking an exact time

Three ways to replace the plain date-time field behind “More times”. They all share one model and the same defaults:

  • Today: an hour from now, rounded up to the next 5 minutes.
  • Any later day: 8:00 AM.
  • Once you set a time yourself, changing the day keeps it. “Back to the default” brings the default back.

A. Two-step dialog closest to the reference

“Pick an exact time” opens a dialog. Pick the day, then the time on a clock: tap or drag an hour and it moves on to the minutes. The keyboard button swaps the clock for typed hours and minutes.

C. Compact: day chips and a two-ring clock

The next week is one tap away as chips; “Other day” opens the month. The clock has hours inside and minutes outside, both live, so there's no switching between them. The time box takes typing too: “3”, “330p” or “15:30”.

Pick an exact time

Drag the inner ring for the hour and the outer ring for the minutes. On the clock, up and down change the hour; left and right change the minutes.

Open questions

  • Should a time you set carry to a new day, as it does here, or should every change of day go back to the default?
  • Late at night, an hour from now runs past midnight, so picking Today lands on tomorrow at 12-something AM. Try it with “Pretend it’s”.
  • The clock moves in 5-minute steps. Typing (A’s keyboard button, C’s time box) takes any minute.
