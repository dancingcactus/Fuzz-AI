Retro pals

Two more old machines for Fuzz's family: a boombox and a rotary phone, each in two designs, with a few moves. Neither has arms, so they test how far a gimmick (speakers, tape reels, a handset, a curly cord) can carry a character. Click any pal to replay the move.

The family

Fuzz, Spike and the new pals at the same scale. Party starts everyone on the same beat.

Notes

  • Same family rules. The same face as Fuzz and Spike, the warm foundation colours, a dark ink outline and a soft ground shadow. The boombox is cream with Fuzz's terracotta and green keys; the phone is honey.
  • Same shape as Spike. One SVG per pal, named groups, one CSS animation per group, pivots in viewBox px, no ids. pals.css and pals.js stand alone.
  • Mood signals. Boombox: the speakers pump, the reels turn (backwards to rewind), the antenna shoots up, notes float out. Phone: the handset rattles, lifts like a hat or slams, the dial turns, the cord jiggles.
